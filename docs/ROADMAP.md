# Milestones

Each milestone runs, is tested at eye height and on a phone, and is committed before the next.

| # | Milestone | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Setup and deploy** | Vite + three.js scene, Worker with `/api/health`, auto-deploy from GitHub to Cloudflare. |
| 1 | **Landscape** | Copernicus DEM terrain around the site, river (marked "inferred"), sky, fog, fly-over camera (desktop + touch). |
| 2a | **The city (rules)** | Crisp platforms, main streets and drains, ~2,000 generated courtyard houses, the Great Bath, Great Hall, pillared hall; every element tagged excavated / inferred / imagined. |
| 2b | **The city (traced)** | A local tracing page; walls of the excavated areas (HR, VS, DK, SD) traced from Marshall 1931 / Mackay 1938 replace generated houses there. |
| 3 | **Certainty layer + info panel** | One control: "alive" / "evidence". Click any feature: what it was, what we know, how sure, sources. |
| 4 | **Walk mode** | First person at eye height, WASD + touch, collision, minimap. |
| 5 | **Time of day and seasons** | Sun path calculated for the site in four seasons, dawn to night, stars, warm night lamps (imagined). One control. |
| 6 | **Monsoon** | Rain, wet streets and walls, water running along the street drains, the river rising and turning silty in the flood season. |
| 7 | **Life in the city** | Simple figures at attested crafts (beads, shell, pottery, seals, spinning, metal, grinding, wells) and people in the streets; click a workshop for its note. |
| 8 | **City through time** | Five moments (founding, height, late city, buried with the stupa, excavated today) in one control; DK-G uses Mackay's Late plan for the late city; then-and-now split view. |
| 9 | **Indus script** | An explorer: an example seal as carved and as impressed, common signs (redrawn), facts in numbers, the main proposals shown as proposals; linked from the seal carver. |
| 10 | **Sound** | Generative sound made live with Web Audio (no recordings): wind, river, rain and thunder, birds, crickets, people, workshop taps, an imagined music; one on/off button. |
| 11 | **Talk to a resident** | Chat with an imagined resident (three to choose from) through the Worker and Gemini; answers only from the app's own notes, honest "nobody knows"; key stays on the server. |
| 12 | **Guide** | Ten-step tour, list of buildings, search. |
| 13 | **Polish** | Hover labels, link-preview image and meta tags, phone tuning, credits screen. |
